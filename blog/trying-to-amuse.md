A couple of years back I stumbled across a blog article on the topic of starting a blog. The post was written as a guide to a friend looking to start a blog and at the time it was really something I was thinking about. It has been a while since that article planted the idea in my head but I finally pulled something together. The line that stands out to me most from the post is "So, don’t think too much about how you are supposed to write, how others do it, or what the conventions demand. **Just try to amuse yourself**." The last sentance especially stands out. I don't know what I am going to write here, but it will make sense to me.

I am writing this while I am watching [Lost in Translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_in_Translation) for the first time. I was only reminded of the movie after one of the [Mr. Kitty After Dark](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKgtA3h8WB8) remake videos popped up on a random YouTube playlist. I am not entirely sure why I never watched it. The pacing of he movie is so deliberately slow, and the expressions on Bill Murray's face throughout really are amazing. He has these looks where you can really feel the *what am I doing here*, *what am I doing with my life* and see him lost in a thought that never really forms. Stuck somewhere between talking to the kids and wanting to eat more Japanese food. If you haven't ever watched this movie, don't be like me and wait (it is from 2003). I will give the movie a solid 9 out of 10.
